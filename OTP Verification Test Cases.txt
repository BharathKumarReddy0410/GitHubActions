OTP Verification Test Cases
----------------------------------------
Functional Test Cases
------------------------
Verify OTP is generated and sent to the registered email/mobile number.
Verify the OTP length matches the requirement (e.g., 6 digits).
Verify login is successful when a valid OTP is entered.
Verify an appropriate error message is displayed for an invalid OTP.
Verify an expired OTP cannot be used.
Verify a previously used OTP cannot be reused.
Verify OTP is sent to the correct user.
Verify OTP validation time is as per requirements.
Verify a new OTP is generated when "Resend OTP" is clicked.
Verify only the latest OTP is valid after multiple resend requests.
Verify OTP generation is blocked after maximum resend attempts.
Verify maximum invalid attempts are limited.
Verify appropriate error messages are displayed.


Boundary Test Cases
--------------------------
Verify entering OTP with fewer digits than required.
Verify entering OTP with more digits than required.
Verify entering the exact valid length OTP.
Verify OTP field accepts only numeric characters.
Verify alphabets and special characters are rejected.


Security Test Cases
----------------------
Verify OTP is masked if masking is a requirement.
Verify OTP cannot be used on another device/session after successful verification.
Verify OTP cannot be used after logout.
Verify OTP cannot be reused after timeout.
Verify brute-force attempts are blocked after maximum failures.


Network and Reliability Test Cases
-----------------------------------------------
Verify OTP delivery under slow network conditions.
Verify behavior when the network disconnects while entering OTP.
Verify behavior when the system/browser is closed during OTP verification.
Verify whether login needs to be restarted after browser refresh.
Verify OTP delivery time meets SLA requirements.


Cross Browser and Session Test Cases
-----------------------------------------------
Verify OTP screen works across multiple browsers.
Verify multiple tabs handling.
Verify session timeout during OTP entry.
Verify Back and Refresh button behavior.

Resend OTP
-------------------
Verify "Resend OTP" button is disabled for a few seconds to prevent abuse.
Verify countdown timer is displayed correctly.
Verify clicking Resend OTP multiple times quickly doesn't send duplicate OTPs.

Copy/Paste
-----------------
Verify OTP can be pasted into the field.
Verify spaces before or after OTP are trimmed.
Verify auto-focus moves between OTP boxes.

Empty Field Validation
---------------------------
Verify clicking Verify without entering OTP.
Verify partially entered OTP cannot be submitted.

Accessibility
------------------------
Verify OTP field supports keyboard navigation.
Verify screen readers can access the OTP field.

Performance
---------------
Verify OTP generation under high load.
Verify simultaneous OTP requests from multiple users.

Notification
---------------
Verify SMS/email content contains the correct OTP.
Verify no duplicate notifications are received.

Session Management
----------------------
Verify session expires after OTP timeout.
Verify refreshing the page doesn't bypass OTP verification.
Verify user is redirected correctly after successful verification.
